# Usability Test Script

Before finalizing the launch, run this exact test script with 5 different people to ensure the portfolio meets its core goals (memorable, fast, accessible).

## Test Subjects
You need 5 participants:
- 2 Recruiters (or people with hiring experience)
- 2 Designers (for feedback on typography and polish)
- 1 Non-technical friend

## The Script

### 1. The 30-Second Drill
**Prompt:** "Open this link. You have 30 seconds to find what kind of projects I build. Go."
**What to observe:** 
- Do they scroll straight to the Rolling Mill?
- Do they notice or use the Control Room sliders?
- Can they read the project cards easily?
- Did they get confused by the layout?

### 2. The Contact Check
**Prompt:** "Imagine you want to interview me tomorrow. How would you contact me?"
**What to observe:**
- Do they use the sticky header, or scroll all the way down to the Dispatch section?
- Is the tap target for the email/phone reveal large enough on their mobile device?
- Is there any friction in copying the email?

### 3. The Memory Test (After closing the tab)
**Prompt:** "Close the tab. What is the one thing you remember about the site?"
**What to observe:**
- Did the "Forged in Bokaro" theme land?
- Did they remember the heat line or the molten aesthetics?
- Did they feel it was unique compared to standard portfolios?

## Success Criteria
- Recruiters find projects and contact info in < 15 seconds.
- The site feels memorable (the steel plant narrative).
- No usability complaints on mobile (fat-finger issues, unreadable text).
